About Universal Media Player

A single-file media player with local playback, playlists, subtitles, picture-in-picture, direct media downloads, and official social-platform embeds.

Important: YouTube and Instagram playback is provided through their official embedded experiences. This page cannot bypass private posts, age restrictions, login requirements, geo-blocks, ads, or creator-disabled embedding. It does not download or extract protected streams.
